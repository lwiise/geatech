Netlify forms — self check

Diagnostics page for the site owner. It is not linked from anywhere on the site and search engines are asked to skip it.

This sends one test submission to each of the three forms (contact, newsletter, footer) and tells you exactly what is wrong if they are not reaching Netlify.

You will get three notification e-mails (that is the point — it proves they work) and three rows marked NETLIFY CHECK in Forms, which you can delete afterwards. It also uses 3 of your 100 free monthly submissions.

Open this page on the deployed Netlify site, not from the files on your computer.
