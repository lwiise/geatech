Form definitions

Nothing to do here. This page exists so that Netlify can find the site's three forms while it deploys.

The real forms live on the contact page, the home page and in the footer. They are written in Webflow's exported markup, which Netlify's form detector does not always manage to read. The copies below say the same thing in the plainest possible HTML, so detection cannot miss them: Netlify merges form definitions by name, so contact, newsletter and footer get registered either way.

Keep the field names here in step with the real forms — see NETLIFY_SETUP.md.
